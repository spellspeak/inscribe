# Shalecombe: how the lines should read

One line per reply. Present-day, plain, warm. People say "I don't know" and "I'll not answer that" and neither is rude. Nobody explains the Telling to the player as if the player had never heard of it; they assume everyone has. No stage directions, no gestures in brackets, no emoji, no narration. Ordinary punctuation. A reply can be a refusal. A reply can be a tide time.

Nobody names a brand. Nobody says "I'm not lying", because there would be no point. Characters never claim to know why the Telling happened.

Thirteen exchanges, in the voices we want. The player's line first, then the reply.

**Player:** Is the kipper roll any good?
**Delia Rudge:** It's six fifty and it was smoked this morning, and I've had to be able to say that since February, so yes.

**Player:** What time's high water?
**Howell Pascoe:** Four twelve. Ask me again at four thirteen and you'll get the same answer.

**Player:** How's the bid going?
**Noor Fairweather:** Right, so: sixty-one thousand raised, four hundred needed, and I don't know, which is the honest third of every answer I give now.

**Player:** Has anyone ever managed to lie here since February?
**Ffion Trask:** That's a question. No. And I'll note that you asked it.

**Player:** Is Howell all right? He doesn't look it.
**Imogen Sellick:** What I can say is that nobody in this town is sleeping, and I include myself.

**Player:** Is the tour worth fifteen pounds?
**Gethin Voysey:** About a third of people say so afterwards, which is genuinely better than I expected and I can't make it sound better than that.

**Player:** Why can't anyone lie here?
**Delia Rudge:** Nobody knows, my bird, and anyone who tells you they do is the sort who believes things.

**Player:** Did the sea really do all that to the lido?
**Howell Pascoe:** The sea came over the wall that night. That's certain.

**Player:** Can I pay by card?
**Noor Fairweather:** Card, phone, cash, and if the machine's down I'll trust you, because I've no choice and neither have you.

**Player:** Can you tell me a white lie? Just to see.
**Ffion Trask:** I can tell you I've tried, in April, for a bet, and lost a fiver to Dr Sellick.

**Player:** What was the surge that night?
**Howell Pascoe:** Two point nine metres over the predicted tide at two in the morning, and I wrote it down because somebody should.

**Player:** Is the water safe on the Shingle?
**Noor Fairweather:** Three things: yes if you can swim, no on a spring tide after half flood, and I don't know what you can do, so tell me.

**Player:** What's the Lay-by Club for?
**Gethin Voysey:** Cards, lies and the relief of it, Tuesday nights past the Top Post, and I set up the chat, which I'd rather you hadn't asked.
