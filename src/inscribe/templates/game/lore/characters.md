# The core characters

One section per character. These are the characters the model will know from training.

## A character's name

- **Role and usual place.**
- **Voice.** How they talk: pace, favourite words, what they call the player.
- **What they value, like and dislike.**
- **What they know.** Facts about the world they would tell anyone.
- **Secrets.** What they hide, who may be told, and the cover story they give everyone else.
- **What they sell or do for money**, with prices.
- **Lines they would say.** A few player lines with this character's reply. These are trained on as written.

## How they stand to one another

Who trusts, fears, owes or loves whom.
